# Revised Capstone Project Sections

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Title:** Optimizing Sales Performance and Delivery Efficiency Through Data Analytics

**Project Category:** Business Intelligence and Operational Analytics

**Industry:** Retail and E-commerce

**Data Source:** Kaggle (dataset to be confirmed)

**Primary Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

**Project Duration:** 3–4 weeks

**Main Deliverable:** An interactive Power BI dashboard supported by documented findings and actionable business recommendations.

## 2. Analytical Tools and Environment

Microsoft Power BI will serve as the primary environment for data preparation, analysis, visualization, and reporting.

The project will use Power Query within Power BI to clean and transform the dataset, establish appropriate data relationships, and prepare the data for analysis. DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) will be used to create calculated measures and KPIs, while Power BI's visualization and filtering capabilities will support interactive exploration of business performance.

The analytical workflow will include:

- Importing the selected dataset into Power BI.
- Inspecting and transforming data using Power Query.
- Handling missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent data formats.
- Establishing relationships between tables where necessary.
- Creating DAX measures for business KPIs.
- Developing interactive charts, KPI cards, and filters.
- Validating calculations and dashboard results.
- Interpreting findings and developing business recommendations.

## 3. Revised Project Methodology

### Phase 1: Business Understanding and Data Acquisition

Define the business problem, establish research questions, identify required KPIs, and obtain a suitable retail transaction dataset from Kaggle.

**Output:** Project brief and selected dataset.

### Phase 2: Data Preparation

Import the dataset into Power BI and use Power Query to assess data quality, correct inconsistencies, handle missing values, and prepare the data for analysis.

**Output:** Cleaned and analysis-ready data model.

### Phase 3: Data Analysis

Use Power BI visuals, DAX measures, filters, and comparative analysis to investigate sales trends, product performance, delivery outcomes, and channel profitability.

**Output:** Analytical findings and validated KPI calculations.

### Phase 4: Interactive Dashboard Development

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring relevant KPIs, performance trends, product comparisons, sales-channel analysis, and shipping and delivery metrics.

**Output:** Functional Power BI dashboard.

### Phase 5: Interpretation and Recommendations

Interpret the findings, identify areas requiring improvement, and develop evidence-based recommendations supported by the analysis.

**Output:** Documented insights and business recommendations.

### Phase 6: Validation and Final Presentation

Validate dashboard calculations, check data consistency, review the research questions, and present the completed dashboard alongside the key findings and recommendations.

**Output:** Final capstone presentation and completed Power BI dashboard.

## 5. Final Project Deliverables

The primary deliverable will be a **fully interactive Power BI dashboard** designed to support business decision-making.

The dashboard will contain:

1. **Executive Overview:** Revenue, gross profit, profit margin, order volume, and overall performance indicators.
2. **Sales and Product Performance:** Sales trends, product contributions, and profitability comparisons.
3. **Shipping and Delivery Analysis:** Shipping status, delivery performance, and delay trends.
4. **Sales Channel Performance:** Comparison of revenue, profitability, order volume, and delivery performance across channels.
5. **Interactive Filtering:** Filters and slicers for available dimensions such as year, product, and sales channel.
6. **Business Insights and Recommendations:** Key findings and proposed actions derived from the analysis.

Supporting deliverables will include:

- A Power BI project file (`.pbix`).
- A concise project summary documenting the business problem, research questions, KPIs, major findings, limitations, and recommendations.
- The dataset citation and source URL.
- A final presentation demonstrating the dashboard and explaining its business value.

## 6. Revised Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- The dashboard addresses the defined business questions.
- All KPI calculations are accurate and validated.
- Users can interact with the dashboard through relevant filters and slicers.
- The visualizations communicate findings clearly.
- The analysis identifies meaningful performance patterns or explains when the available data is insufficient.
- Recommendations are supported by evidence from the analysis.
- The Power BI project file is organized and ready for demonstration or assessment.
